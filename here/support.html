Here – Support

Help for the Here safety check-in app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Contact

Email mic.bur@email.cz. Include the app version, device model and a short description of the problem. Do not include health information, precise location or other sensitive data.

Pairing

Open Mutual Monitoring and display My Code on one device. On the other device, tap Add Contact and scan or enter the code. Both devices need an internet connection and notification permission.

Notifications

Open iOS Settings → Notifications → Here and enable notifications. Time-sensitive notifications can be enabled separately.

Apple Watch

Verify that Here Watch is installed in the Watch app and that Health permissions are enabled. Heart-rate monitoring requires a supported Apple Watch worn on the wrist.

Account and data deletion

Open Here → Settings → Delete Account. This removes the local profile and requests deletion of associated server data.

Emergency disclaimer

Here does not replace emergency services. Contact local emergency services immediately when urgent help is required.
