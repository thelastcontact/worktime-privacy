Here – Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Here is a personal safety and check-in application. This policy covers the iPhone app, Apple Watch app, widgets, mutual monitoring, safety alerts and SOS features.

Data we process

  • Your display name, internal account identifier and device notification token.
  • Names and phone numbers of trusted contacts you provide.
  • Check-in status, schedules, travel information and content you enter.
  • Heart-rate and activity information when you grant Health and Motion access.
  • Precise location while an SOS location-sharing feature is active.
  • Subscription status and technical diagnostics required to operate the service.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide check-ins, mutual monitoring, reminders, alerts, SOS location sharing, subscriptions, technical operation and support. Health and fitness data is never used for advertising, marketing or data brokerage.

Storage and providers

Settings are stored on your device. Features that synchronize safety status, contacts, notifications or SOS information also store the minimum necessary data on secured cloud infrastructure. Necessary data may be processed by Apple services, cloud hosting and notification providers, and messaging providers when those features are used.

We do not sell personal data and do not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Retention and deletion

Server data is retained while needed to provide the service or comply with legal obligations. Delete your account and associated server data in Here → Settings → Delete Account. Local information can be removed by deleting the app.

Permissions

Here requests access only when a related feature needs it. Permissions can be reviewed or revoked in iOS Settings. Some functionality will be unavailable after permission is revoked.

Security and safety

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Here is an assistance tool and does not replace emergency services.

Children

Here is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect children’s information without appropriate authorization.
